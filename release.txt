The first round (22–24/10/2026, commit 0231fef) gave 82 passes and 9 failures, and the team's re-run on Windows (Node.js v24.22.1) matched it. The final re-run on this release fixed BL-10 and BL-11 (and `npm ci` now works, DEF-01) and found one new failure, BL-08 (DEF-15). The team repeated it on Windows on 31/10/2026 with the same results.

Manual testing (26–30/10/2026; the 26/10 tests were on the earlier commit 0231fef): Edge and Firefox (MAN-01), Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra in Chrome (MAN-02), live AI search and map (MAN-04) and keyboard-only use (MAN-05) all passed. On the live site, an enquiry with a photo reached the client's Gmail (MAN-03), and HTTPS, robots.txt, the sitemap and the 404 page were checked (MAN-06). A usability test with 3 real participants (USA-01) completed all tasks and produced 4 findings (U1–U4).

Client acceptance: Sonny Rolih accepted the website in the recorded team meeting on 27/10/2026 and signed it off in writing by email on 30/10/2026.
